Phone says "Connection Interrupted" or Cannot Connect?

  • Same Wi-Fi Network: Verify your phone is connected to the exact same Wi-Fi as your laptop (not mobile data / 5G).
  • Windows Defender Firewall: If your network is set to "Public" in Windows, Windows blocks incoming connections from other devices. Run allow-firewall.bat as Administrator to unblock port 8080.
  • USB Cable Mode (Zero Wi-Fi Required): Plug in your phone via USB-C with USB Debugging enabled, choose localhost (USB ADB) in the dropdown above, and open http://localhost:8080 on your phone!
